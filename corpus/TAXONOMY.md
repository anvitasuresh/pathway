# Construct Taxonomy

Constructs for the benchmark, adapted from Meloy's TRAP-18 and the Secret Service NTAC
pathway-to-violence literature (`CONV` is original to this project):

- `FIX` — fixation
- `IDN` — identification
- `LEAK` — leakage
- `RHRS` — rehearsal
- `ESC` — escalation
- `CONV` — reality convergence (motivated by the Acton MA case, CLAUDE.md §2.2)
